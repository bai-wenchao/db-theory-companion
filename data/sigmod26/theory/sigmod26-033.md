## Statements
**Definition Budget-Aware Entity Matching Across Domains (EMAD).** Given a domain partition of $N = n_1 n_2 n_j$ over the domains $ D $ of the candidate set and a training budget $β$, the goal of EMAD is, for each domain $i D $, to construct a budgeted training set \[ X_i = n_i^ * S_i^ out , where |X_i| = β, S_i^ out (N n_i), \] such that the resulting model achieves high matching performance on domain $i$.
