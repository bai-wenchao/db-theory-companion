## Statements
**Theorem .** For a set $Q$ of queries that are uniformly distributed in the hypersphere centered at $x$ with radius $l_m$, $$ L(c',c,Q) ||r'||^2 + λ r^T r' $$ where $r=c-x$, $r'=c'-x$, and $λ>0$ is a constant factor.

## Proofs
**Proof.** Let $Q_l$ be the set of queries that are uniformly distributed over the hypersphere centered at $x$ with radius $l$. We may expand the expectation as follows: \[ & L(c', c, Q_l) = E_ ||R||=l [ ReLU (- α) · (||q-c'||^2-||q-x||^2)] \\ &= _ 2 ^ - α\ E_ ||R||=l [(R-r')^2-R^2\ |\ R,r ||R||·||r|| = α]\\ &\ \ dP( R,r ||R||·||r|| < α) \] To evaluate this integral, we decompose $R$ and $r'$ into components parallel and orthogonal to $r$. As shown in Figure~ [ref] , we call these components $R_ $, $R_ $, $r_ '$, $r_ '$, respectively. Also, we let $θ= cxc'$, $β$ is the angle between $R_ $ and $r_ '$. Then we simplify this inner expectation given fixed $r$, $r'$, $θ$, and $α$: \[ & E_ ||R||=l [(R-r')^2-R^2\ |\ R,r ||R||·||r|| = α] \\ &= E_ ||R||=l [(R_ -r_ ')^2 + (R_ -r_ ')^2 - R_ ^2 - R_ ^2\ |\ ||R_ || = l α] \\ &= E_ ||R||=l [r_ '(r_ '-2R_ ) + r_ '^2-2r_ 'R_ \ |\ ||R_ || = l α] \\ &= E_ ||R||=l [||r'|| θ (||r'|| θ - 2l α) + ||r'||^2 ^2 θ - 2r_ 'R_ \ |\\ & ||R_ || = l α] \\ &= E_ β [||r'||^2 - 2l||r'|| θ α - 2l||r'|| θ α β] \\ &= ||r'|| · (||r'|| - 2l θ α - 2l θ α E[ β]) \\ &= ||r'|| · (||r'|| - 2l θ α) \] Meanwhile, $dP( R,r ||R||·||r|| < α)$ is proportional to the surface area of a $(D-1)$-dimensional hypersphere of radius $l α$. Thus, we express it as $A ^ D-2 α$ for some constant $A$. Our integral then becomes \[ L(c', c, Q_l) &= _ 2 ^ - α ||r'|| (||r'|| - 2l θ α) A ^ D-2 α dα \\ &= - A||r'||^2 _ 2 ^ α ^ D-2 α dα \\ & +\ 2Al||r'|| θ _ 2 ^ ^2 α ^ D-2 α dα \\ &= A||r'||^2 D-1 \ +\ Al||r'|| θ _ 0 ^ ( ^ D-2 α - ^ D α) dα \] Now we define $I_D = _ 0 ^ ^D α dα$. \[ I_D &= 2 _ 0 ^ /2 ^D α dα \\ &= -2 α ^ D-1 α\ |_ 0 ^ /2 \\ & -2 _ 0 ^ /2 α (-(D-1) α ^ D-2 α) dα \\ &= 2(D-1) _ 0 ^ /2 ^2 α ^ D-2 α dα \\ &= (D-1)I_ D-2 - (D-1)I_D \] Combining terms, we have $I_D = D-1 D I_ D-2 $. Then the loss satisfies: \[ L(c', c, Q_l) &= A||r'||^2 D-1 \ +\ Al||r'|| θ (I_ D-2 - I_D) \\ &= A||r'||^2 D-1 \ +\ Al||r'|| θ I_D D-1 \\ &= A D-1 (||r'||^2 + I_Dl θ ||r'||) \\ &= A D-1 (||r'||^2 + I_Dl ||r|| r^T r') \\ \] Note that the hypersphere $Q$ consists of many hyperspherical surface $Q_l$. We solve $L(c',c,Q)$: \[ L(c', c, Q) &= _ 0 ^ l_m L(c', c, Q_l) · Dl^ D-1 l_m^D dl \\ &= _ 0 ^ l_m A D-1 (||r'||^2 + I_Dl ||r|| r^T r') Dl^ D-1 l_m^D dl \\ &= A D-1 (||r'||^2 + _ 0 ^ l_m D I_D l^ D l_m^D||r|| r^T r' dl) \\ &= A D-1 (||r'||^2 + DI_Dl_m (D+1)||r|| r^T r') \\ & ||r'||^2 + λ r^T r' \] where $λ = DI_Dl_m (D+1)||r|| > 0$.
