**Theorem Aggregation Error Bound for Value-Sensitive Queries.** Let \( agg _S\) denote the approximate aggregate returned by for a value-sensitive query (e.g., \( AVG \), \( VAR \)). Given user-specified tolerances \( _S\) and \( _ NN \), and confidence level \(1-α\), the approximation error satisfies \[ [ | agg _S - agg _D | ≤ _S + _ NN ] ≥ 1-α, \] provided that the sample size \(s\) and pilot sample size \(s_p\) satisfy \[ s ≥ _1· 1 _S^2 , s_p ≥ _2· 1 λ^2 , \] where \(λ\) is a configurable tolerance on the difference between \( F1 _S\) and \( F1 _ S_p \) and \( _ NN = C_1·λ + C_2·λ^2. \) Here, \( _1, _2, C_1,\) and \(C_2\) depend on the specific aggregation function as follows: ; For \( AVG \), \[ _1 &= (b-a)^2ln(2/α) 2 , _2=32ln(2/α),\\ C_1 &= 2(b-a) |ON_S| , C_2=0. \] ; For \( VAR \), \[ _1 &= (b-a)^4ln(2/α) 2 , _2=32ln(2/α),\\ C_1&= 3(b-a)^2 |ON_S| , C_2= (b-a)^2 |ON_S| . \] where \(a\) and \(b\) are the lower and upper bounds of the target attribute ; \( = |ON_S|/|S|\) is the the neighborhood density.

**Theorem Aggregation Error Bound for \(\mathtt{SUM}\) Queries.** Let \( SUM _S\) denote the approximate aggregate returned by Two-Phase for the \( SUM \) query. Given user-specified tolerances \( _S\) and \( _ NN \), and confidence level \(1-α\), the approximation error satisfies \[ [ | SUM _S - SUM _D | ≤ _S + _ NN ] ≥ 1-α, \] provided that the sample size \(s\) and pilot sample size \(s_p\) satisfy \[ s ≥ max (s^ count ,\ s^ avg ), s_p ≥ max (s_p^ count ,\ s_p^ avg ). \] Here: ; \(s^ count = 2|D|^2| AVG _S|^2ln(4/α) _S^2 \) bounds the sampling error on the neighbor count. ; \(s^ avg = 2(b-a)^2|ON_D|^2ln(4/α) _S^2 \) bounds the sampling error on the attribute mean. ; \(s_p^ count = _2^ COUNT · 1 ( _ NN ^ SUM - · _C )^2 \) bounds the count selection error, where \( _ NN ^ SUM = _ NN |S| 2|D|| AVG _S| \) and \( = |ON_S| s \) denotes the neighborhood density in \(S\). ; \(s_p^ avg = _2^ AVG · 1 λ^2 \) bounds the mean selection error, where \(| F1 _S- F1 _ S_p |≤λ\).
